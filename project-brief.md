# Project Brief

## Project Identity

**furventures** — a portfolio site for a furry-convention-focused F&B (food & beverage) business.

## One-Sentence Concept

A one-page portfolio site for furventures that explains what its furry-convention F&B offerings are, shares a little of the story and history, and showcases example work — so visitors quickly understand the business and see what it offers.

## Target User

Everyone — potential customers, investors/partners, and the wider community who visit the site.

## User Goal

Visitors should be able to:
1. Understand furventures' furry-convention F&B offerings and story.
2. See examples of the work/portfolio, clearly presented.

Primary action: **View the portfolio / examples of work.**

## Build Shape

Content-led site.

## Shape Confirmation

Confirmed by the learner: "Yes, content-led site is correct." The main value is reading, navigating, and understanding the business and its work — not creating or editing data.

## Version-One Success

"Done enough" when the site has:
- A homepage that explains the business and shows a few example projects.
- An About section with placeholder story and history.
- A portfolio grid with short descriptions of examples, nicely laid out.
- A footer with a contact option, plus placeholder email and phone details.
- Themed visuals (paw mark, illustration tiles, warm con ambiance) that fit the furry-convention F&B identity.
- Responsive layout that works on mobile.
- Honest contact behavior (no backend — sample/mailto only).

## Now / Later / Never

### Now

- One-page content-led site.
- Hero/business intro section with a themed visual.
- About section with placeholder story and history.
- Portfolio grid with sample examples and short descriptions.
- Footer with contact option, placeholder email + phone, and contact form.
- Themed visuals (inline SVG paw mark, emoji/illustrated card tiles, warm accent).
- Responsive mobile-friendly layout.
- Working links and honest form behavior.

### Later

- Multiple pages.
- Real client testimonials or reviews.
- Live menu or ordering.
- Blog or news section.

### Never

- No login.
- No payments.
- No database.
- No backend.
- No live APIs or secret keys.
- No invented/fake project examples — samples must be clearly labelled.
- No fake testimonials, fake stats, or invented real numbers.
- Placeholder content must be clearly marked as placeholder (including placeholders like email/phone).
- No multiple pages or complex navigation in version one.

## Assumptions

- The portfolio examples will be real furventures work, or clearly-labelled sample items.
- The contact option will be a mailto link or a sample "thank you" message — no backend sending.
- Placeholder contact details are hello@furventures.example and +60 12-345 6789, clearly labelled "Placeholder", until real ones are provided.
- About/story/history content is placeholder and will be replaced by real copy later.
- Deployment target is GitHub + Vercel (accounts confirmed and logged in).

## Proof Target

A responsive one-page portfolio site for furventures, deployed on Vercel, viewable on desktop and mobile, with working navigation links and an honest contact action.
