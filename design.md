# Design Direction

## Design Inspiration URL

https://designmd.ai/faruqqqz/manuvox-rencana-desain-ui-lengkap-per-layar

This URL is **inspiration only** — a reference for visual direction, not a clone target.

## What We Borrow

- Layout: Single-column content flow on mobile; centered max-width container (about 1080px) on desktop; clear vertical section rhythm.
- Spacing: Generous white space between sections; consistent section padding (about 64px–96px vertical) so nothing feels crowded.
- Color mood: Light, airy background with a calm base; one deep accent color for headings/buttons; warm support tone for highlights.
- Typography feel: Clean, modern sans-serif with strong heading weights and a readable body size (16px+).
- Card/button style: Rounded corners (12–16px), soft subtle borders, one strong filled primary button, quiet ghost/secondary buttons.
- Mobile feel: Content stacks vertically, taps are large (min 44px touch targets), first screen shows header + hero with no horizontal scroll.

## What We Do Not Copy

- Brand/logo: Do not copy the reference brand name, logo, or identity. Ours is **furventures**.
- Content/copy: Do not copy any text, taglines, or descriptions from the reference.
- Testimonials/stats: No fake testimonials or invented metrics. Any stats must be clearly marked sample.
- Photos/private identity: No copying photos, people, or private identity from the reference.
- Exact layout: Do not replicate the reference screen-by-screen. Only the general mood, spacing feel, and component polish carry over.

## Visual Mood

Official, premium, calm, bold accents — a trustworthy F&B business that feels polished and modern without being loud.

## Layout Rules

- One page, 5 sections in order: Header / Hero / About / Portfolio / Footer.
- Hero (first screen): business name large and bold, one-line intro under it, one clear primary action button ("View Our Work") that smooth-scrolls to the portfolio.
- Section rhythm: consistent vertical padding between sections; each section starts with an h2 and a short intro line.
- Portfolio: responsive grid — 1 column on small phones, 2 on tablet, 3–4 on desktop; cards are equal height with consistent internal padding.
- Footer: contact form centered with clear labels, plus small copyright line.
- Primary action: one — "View Our Work" in the hero; navigation links are secondary.

## Color / Contrast Rules

- Background: off-white / very light neutral (keeps it calm and readable).
- Text: near-black dark neutral (contrast ratio well above 4.5:1 on the background).
- Accent: one deep, appetizing accent color (e.g., deep warm red/terracotta or rich brown) for headings, primary button, and small highlights — used sparingly.
- Support/alt: one muted warm tone for soft section backgrounds or card fills.
- Ensure all text meets readable contrast; no light-gray text on white.

## Typography Feel

Official-premium with calm readability: clean sans-serif for body text; bold, slightly tighter letter-spacing for headings. Body minimum 16px; headings scale clearly (h1 largest, h2 section titles, h3 card titles). No decorative or handwritten fonts in version one.

## Component Style

- Buttons: primary = filled accent color, white text, rounded (12–16px), comfortable padding; secondary/ghost = outlined or quiet with clear hover state; min 44px tap height.
- Cards: white or very-light fill, subtle border, rounded corners (12–16px), soft shadow only when it helps separation (no glow/heavy shadow).
- Inputs: full-width on mobile, clear border, visible label above each field, comfortable padding, visible focus outline.
- Status chips / labels: small rounded pill with muted background; used to clearly mark portfolio items as "Sample".

## Mobile Rules

- First screen on phone: compact header + hero (business name, one-line intro, primary button); no horizontal scroll.
- Sections stack vertically; grid becomes 1 column on small phones.
- Nav links stay simple and tappable (smooth scroll, min 44px hit area).
- Form fields full-width; button full-width on phones.
- No fixed/overlay elements that block content on mobile.

## Accessibility Basics

- Healthy contrast between text and backgrounds.
- Visible focus states on links, buttons, and inputs.
- All form fields have clear visible labels.
- Tap targets at least 44px.
- Semantic markup: one h1, h2 per section, h3 for card titles.
- Text resizes without breaking layout.

## Anti-Slop Rules

- No fake logos.
- No fake testimonials.
- No fake stats unless clearly marked sample.
- No lorem ipsum in final proof.
- No random gradient blobs or generic AI decoration.
- No heavy shadows, glows, or animations just for effect.
- One clear primary action (View Our Work).
- Readable on phone width.
- Portfolio items are labelled "Sample" so nothing reads as invented real work.

## Design Verification Checklist

- [ ] The first screen matches the intended mood: official, premium, calm with bold accents.
- [ ] The layout supports the user goal: understand furventures + view the portfolio.
- [ ] Mobile width is readable with no horizontal scroll.
- [ ] Nothing clones the reference brand or identity.
- [ ] The build avoids fake claims, fake stats, and generic AI decoration.
- [ ] Portfolio cards are clearly labelled as samples.
